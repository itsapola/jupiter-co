02 / Philosophy

Technology should be seen.

Somewhere along the way, home tech learned to hide. It went beige, then flat, then invisible, tucked into cabinets and buried in apps. Jupiter Co. builds the opposite: objects with a face, a dial and a reason to stay on the counter.

  1. 01

    In plain sight.

    If it has to be hidden, we haven't finished designing it. Every object earns its place on the counter.

  2. 02

    Show your work.

    Light, sound or a dial tells you what each object is doing at a glance. No mystery states.

  3. 03

    Hands first.

    Every core function works with a dial, a switch or a touch. The app is optional.

03 / The Details

Made to be seen. Designed to be touched.

Brushed aluminum. Amber acrylic that glows from within. Knurled steel dials with real resistance. Every surface is chosen for how it looks under lamplight and how it feels under your thumb.
